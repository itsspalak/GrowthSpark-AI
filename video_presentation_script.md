# Video Presentation Script — GrowthSpark AI: Campaign Lab
**Target duration:** 2–3 minutes. Record your screen while speaking; replace bracketed notes with your live demo.

Hi, I’m presenting GrowthSpark AI — Campaign Lab, a small technology-focused digital marketing prototype built for the AI Growth & Automation Associate assessment.

The problem I wanted to solve is that early-stage teams often need to plan campaigns quickly, but turning a goal and a few customer insights into clear experiments and measurable KPIs can take time. A campaign idea is only useful when a team can test it and understand what success looks like.

I designed GrowthSpark around a simple workflow. The user enters a product or service, target audience, growth goal, marketing channel, budget, test duration, and an optional customer insight. The app then produces campaign angles, three experiment ideas, suggested KPIs, and a practical execution plan. The goal is to help someone move from a broad objective to a testable plan.

For development, I used AI assistance to explore the project idea, define a small MVP, draft the interface and interaction requirements, improve the visual design, and review the documentation for accuracy. I refined the prompts to ask for specific inputs and outputs, actionable experiments, and clear metrics instead of generic marketing suggestions. I also kept the first version dependency-free so it can be reviewed in a browser without an API key.

[SCREEN DEMO: Open the app. Enter a sample product and audience, select a growth goal and channel, choose a budget and test duration, and add a customer insight. Generate the campaign plan. Scroll through the campaign angles, experiments, KPIs, and execution steps.]

One challenge was balancing a useful demonstration with honest claims about the technology. The current version uses predefined JavaScript rules and templates; it does not call a live language model, connect to an advertising platform, or read real campaign analytics. AI tools supported the development process, but the app itself is not yet a live AI-powered service. I’ve documented that limitation clearly.

If I continued this project, I would connect a language model through a secure backend, add safeguards to keep suggestions grounded in the supplied customer insight, and integrate campaign analytics so the team could compare predicted hypotheses with actual results. I would also test the recommendations with real users and track metrics such as experiment completion, conversion rate, and time saved in campaign planning.

The outcome is a working, responsive prototype that demonstrates a growth-planning workflow, structured experimentation, measurable KPIs, and a practical path toward a more advanced solution. Thank you.
